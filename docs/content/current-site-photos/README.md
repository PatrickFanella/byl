# Museum and Fishel Park site-photo review

Issue #85 allows an explicit sourcing gap when reuse permission cannot be
established. This review retains that gap for both places. No photograph is
approved, uploaded, attached or published by this change. The inspected files
remain private research evidence; this repository contains their source records
and hashes, not copies of the images.

The [review manifest](review.json) records the original file/page URLs, known
creator and capture date, missing rights fields, intended place attachment and
existing historical media. Unknown values remain null. A later curator must
complete those fields and approve an accurate caption before import.

These were bounded Park District, HMdb and Commons/name searches. The result
means no inspected lead is cleared, not that no usable photograph exists.
No rights-holder message was sent. A curator can request permission, use an
appropriately licensed original, or commission a new documented exterior view.
Follow [the intake requirements](../INTAKE.md); attribution alone does not grant
reuse rights, and paying for a scan does not establish a reusable license.

The Museum's two stories retain the 1875 Henry Williams Blodgett portrait with
its named painter, collection, CC0 source and resizing note. It is family context,
not a view of the campus or of Israel and Avis. Fishel Park retains the former
school photograph, labelled circa 1915 and explicitly identifying the demolished
building. Neither counts as a present-day site view. The manifest retains their
existing story body hashes, source URLs and complete hero-media metadata for
comparison after a later content release. No historical claim or coordinate is
changed here.

The November 8, 2026 public API review found no place-level photo attachment at
either site; the Museum has separate video/document references. Both hosted
historical JPEGs returned HTTP 200 with image/jpeg content. This is a check of
the existing release, not a new media release. Headless Chromium opened both public place pages at 390 px, found the historical
captions and attribution links, no broken images and no horizontal overflow.
Both screenshots were visually inspected. These checks and their scope are
recorded with the manifest. After a curator eventually publishes a cleared site
image, recheck its hosted response, place Photos gallery, full-size opening,
caption/credit/license/source links and all three retained stories against this
baseline. That future release verification remains pending while the sourcing
gaps remain.
